Digital Subscriber Line : broadband over the existing **telephone line** (twisted pair).

- Variants : **A**symmetric, **S**ymmetric, **V**ery high speed
- Uses [[Frequency Division Multiplexing|FDM]] : phone, upstream and downstream data on separate bands → phone + Internet at the same time
- 20 to 50 users multiplexed by a **DSLAM** (DSL Access Multiplexer) at the local exchange
- "Always on"
- Bitrate drops with distance to the exchange (e.g. ADSL2+ ≥ 8 Mbps for HD IPTV only within ~1 km)
- Dedicated line up to the DSLAM (not shared like [[Hybrid Fiber Coax|HFC]])
