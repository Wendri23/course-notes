**Shared** wireless network connecting end systems to the router via a Base Station (BS) / Access Point (AP).

## Wireless LAN — no roaming
- IEEE 802.11 (WiFi) b/g/n/ac/ax/be... : unlicensed bands (2.4 GHz, 5 GHz)
- IEEE 802.16 (WiMax) : max 70 Mbps per station

## Mobile networks — roaming included
| Gen | Tech | Max per user |
|---|---|---|
| 2G | GPRS | 170 kbps |
| 3G | UMTS/HSDPA | 10 Mbps |
| 4G | LTE / LTE-A | 100 Mbps |
| 5G | NR | 100 Mbps (0.9 GHz) → 20 Gbps (26 GHz) |

Trade-off : higher frequency → more speed, less coverage.
