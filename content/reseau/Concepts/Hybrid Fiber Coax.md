Broadband over the **cable TV** network : fiber up to a node, then coaxial cable to the homes (optical/electrical conversion at the nodes).

- Asymmetric : up to 400 Mbps down / 100 Mbps up (DOCSIS 3.0)
- Uses [[Frequency Division Multiplexing|FDM]] : video channels and data channels on separate bands, "always on"
- **Shared** access to the router among homes → issues : congestion, dimensioning

## DOCSIS 3.0
- Dynamic **channel bonding** : m × 50 Mbps downstream
- More bandwidth thanks to the end of analog TV channels
- New modems, same HFC network — bonding still means sharing
