**Model** : the **cost-effective** use of something in place of something else for some **cognitive purpose** ; something **simpler, safer or cheaper** than reality that we use instead of reality (Rothenberg, 1989).

- a model is an **abstraction** : it cannot represent all aspects of reality, which lets us avoid its complexity, danger and irreversibility
- modelling = **understanding and communicating efficiently** through models ; not drawing, not documentation "because the boss asked", not blindly following conventions
- models are everywhere in engineering : before building, engineers create models and learn from them, to **communicate** with customers, **mitigate risks** and **plan** work (Selic)

## What a model is
A model is…
- a **simplification** of reality…
- …that still captures its **essence**…
- …to help us **understand** it…
- …for a given **usage range and context** (the cognitive purpose).

It must keep some of the original's qualities, so that we can **reason or act** on the system (analysis, generation…) : see [[Model Qualities]].
