**Information system (IS)** : a combination of **hardware, software and telecommunication networks** that **people** build and use to **collect, create and distribute useful data**, typically in organisations (Valacich & Schneider).

- people are part of the IS, not only the machines
- example used throughout the course : an online shop (customers, catalogue, orders, warehouses)

## Models in IS engineering
**Conceptual model** : an **abstract representation of the IS** written in a **standard formalism**.

There are four ways to describe (part of) an IS, from informal to formal : natural language, ad-hoc notations, semi-formal notations, formal notations (see [[Modelling Languages]]). **Conceptual modelling** sits on the semi-formal and formal side.
