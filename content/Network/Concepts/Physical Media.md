## Guided — signal propagates in solid media
- **Twisted pair** : 2 insulated twisted copper wires
- **Coaxial cable** : 2 concentric copper conductors
- **Fiber optics** : glass fiber carrying light pulses
	- low attenuation : 0.3 dB/km (50 % after 10 km) vs 20 dB/km for copper (50 % after 150 m)
	- immune to electromagnetic noise
	- very high-speed point-to-point (undersea cables)

## Unguided — signal propagates freely (radio)
- No physical wire, signal carried in the electromagnetic spectrum
- Propagation effects : reflection, obstruction, interference
- Types : terrestrial microwave (≤ 45 Mbps), WLAN, cellular (≤ 20 Gbps), satellite
- **Satellite** : up to 50 Mbps channel
	- GEO (geostationary) → ~270 ms E2E delay
	- LEO (Low Earth Orbit) → ~30 ms (Starlink, Kuiper, OneWeb)
