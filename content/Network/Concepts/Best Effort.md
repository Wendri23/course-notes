The network does its best to deliver packets, but gives **no guarantee** : no guarantee on delay (how long it takes), nor on delivery (packets can be lost).

- Consequence of [[Packet Switching]] : no resource reservation
- Opposite : [[Circuit Switching]] (guaranteed performance)
